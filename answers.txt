a. Some pros of Agile development are constant value and collaboration for to the customer 
and the ability to have dynamic changes and efficient testing.  Some cons are less timeline
predictability and limited detailed planning/documentation.

b. Scrum masters are responsible for all scrum related tasks like Jira tooling and facilitating
sprints and meetings, while product owners handle releases and stakeholder management, setting
the direction for the team.

c. 1. Sprint Planning Meeting - determine what to complete in the upcoming sprint, 
        determine story points and assign stories to team.
    Daily Scrum Meeting - discuss work completed in the sprint, plan work for the day and
        address blockers.
    Sprint Retrospective Meeting - discuss work completed and backlog incomplete work,
        close the sprint.

d. An epic is a large body of work that is broken into stories, a story is a brief description
of a software system requirement, an issue is any unit of work that can be tracked through the
workflow, a bug is a small unit of work that represents a problem in the code, and a task tracks
work that supports the project but does not add value for the customer.
